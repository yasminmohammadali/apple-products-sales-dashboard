<h1>🍎 Apple Products Sales Dashboard (Power BI)</h1>

<h2>📌 Description</h2>
<p>
This project presents an <b>interactive Power BI dashboard</b> designed to analyze 
<b>Apple product sales, revenue performance, customer ratings, and regional trends</b>.
</p>
<p>
The dashboard provides insights into <b>sales distribution, payment methods, product preferences, and storage trends</b>, 
helping understand business performance and customer behavior.
</p>

<h2>🚀 Key Features</h2>

<h3>🔹 Sales & Revenue Insights</h3>
<ul>
<li>Total sales performance tracking</li>
<li>Revenue by country analysis</li>
<li>Monthly sales trend visualization</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Customer & Rating Analysis</h3>
<ul>
<li>Total ratings overview</li>
<li>Ratings by region</li>
<li>Product-wise rating comparison</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Product Performance</h3>
<ul>
<li>Units sold by product category</li>
<li>Product sales by storage variants</li>
<li>Color-wise product distribution</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Payment Analysis</h3>
<ul>
<li>Sales by payment methods (Apple Pay, Card, Cash, EMI)</li>
<li>Customer payment preferences</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Interactive Filters</h3>
<ul>
<li>Country selection</li>
<li>Payment method filter</li>
<li>Dynamic filtering across all visuals</li>
</ul>

<h2>📊 Dashboard Highlights</h2>
<ul>
<li>Modern and clean UI design</li>
<li>Fully interactive visuals and slicers</li>
<li>Business-focused KPIs</li>
<li>Real-time data exploration</li>
</ul>

<h2>🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used</h2>
